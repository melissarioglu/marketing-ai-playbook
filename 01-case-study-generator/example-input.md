## Nova Apparel — platform deployment, account summary

Company: Nova Apparel (fictional), DTC fashion retailer, UAE and KSA.
Engagement: advertising platform deployment, six months (Mar–Aug 2025).
Interviewees: Layla Haddad, VP Growth; Omar Farouk, Performance Lead.

Challenge: creative production was the launch bottleneck. Campaign
build cycles ran ~3 weeks per market launch, and the team shipped
~40 creative assets per month. Creative was treated as a fixed cost,
not a variable to test.

What was done: Nova migrated its launch workflow onto the platform in
April 2025. Two changes mattered: (1) templated multi-format creative
produced in one build pass; (2) automated budget shifts toward winning
creative within each launch week.

Results, first two quarters:
- Campaign build cycle: ~3 weeks → 12 days per market launch
- Creative output: ~40 → 95 assets/month
- Blended ROAS: 2.1 → 3.4 across the deployment
- CAC: down ~18% by end of Q2
- Time to first live campaign post-migration: 9 days (vs. ~5 weeks historical)

Quote — Layla Haddad, VP Growth: "We stopped treating creative as a
bottleneck and started treating it as a test budget."

Also noted: CTR improved significantly after the first month.
