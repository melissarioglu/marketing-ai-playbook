# Intake Template — what Stage 1 should receive

Garbage in, invented narrative out. If a field is unknown, write
"unknown" — never let anyone guess it into the document.

## Intake fields

- **Company / industry / region:**
- **Engagement & timeline** (what was deployed, start–end):
- **Interviewees** (name, role) **or author of account notes:**
- **Challenge** (in the customer's words where possible):
- **What was actually done** (2-3 moves, dated):
- **Results** (each: value, metric, time period, where it's stated):
- **Quotes** (verbatim, attributed):
- **Caveats** an outsider would need:

## Example of a well-formed results entry

| Value | Metric | Time period | Where stated |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~3 weeks → 12 days | campaign build cycle | per market launch | account summary, results section, line 2 |

Every results entry needs all four. "Improved significantly" with no
value is not a result — it will be excluded by the verification gate.

## Minimum bar before Stage 1

- **5+ sourced metrics**
- **1+ verbatim quote**

Below that, collect more input first — see Troubleshooting #1 in
[prompt-chain.md](prompt-chain.md).
