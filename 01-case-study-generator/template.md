````markdown
# Master Narrative Template — structure Stage 2 must follow

Six sections, fixed order. The rigidity is the feature: every case
study on the site reads the same way, and every section has one job.

## 1. Title
Outcome-first, headline metric in the title.
- ✅ "How Nova Apparel Cut Campaign Build Cycles From 3 Weeks to 12 Days"
- ❌ "Nova Apparel and Us: A Partnership Story"

## 2. Snapshot
One line: company type, industry, region, deployment length.
Exists so a skimming reader knows within 3 seconds whether this case
is relevant to them.

## 3. The challenge
What broke, in the customer's words where possible. The cost of the
problem — time, money, risk — stated with numbers if the evidence
block has them. No problem, no story.

## 4. What we did
2-3 moves maximum, each dated, each explicitly tied to a result in
section 5. If a move has no measurable result behind it, it belongs
in "what's next", not here.

## 5. Results
Every metric from the evidence block, with its time period. Table
format preferred — before/after pairs read fastest. Zero adjectives
doing the work of numbers.

## 6. What's next
Sets up the expansion story: what the customer is testing or planning
next. Also the natural home for [NEEDED: ...] gaps that survived to
draft stage — flag them for the next interview cycle rather than
inventing closure.

---

**Length target:** ~600 words. If it runs long, cut section 4, not
section 5 — results are the part that gets quoted downstream.
````
