Two rules:
1. **Anything outside this sheet is out of scope** — unless the evidence

   The first says what angle to take; the second says what may never
   be claimed.

## C. Negative constraints — do-not-claim list

Starters below; edit per market context. Every line here is enforced
by the supervisor's guardrail check — violations get rejected with the
specific rule that failed.

- No market-size or market-share figure without a dated methodology note
- No competitor pricing without a dated source line
- No growth/momentum adjectives ("fast-growing", "emerging") without a
  snapshot reference
- No regulatory or legal claims without citation
- No claims about our own roadmap
- Gaps become [OPEN QUESTION: ...] — never filled from general knowledge

## D. Section plan (default — edit before freeze)

1. Executive summary *(written last, by the supervisor)*
2. Market & funnel
3. GTM & channels
4. Competitive
5. Pricing
6. Risks & open questions

---

**Done means:** every field in A filled, B numbered, C reviewed line
by line, D frozen. This sheet + the frozen snapshot are the only two
inputs the specialist agents receive.
